Boxlab — what the game actually tests when you press a button

This fighter does not test sprites against sprites. It tests one authored rectangle against the bounding box of the other fighter's pieces, narrowed to a fraction of its width. Everything drawn below is that model, read out of the game's own tables. No artwork is used, or needed.
1Pick a fighter and a move Normals and specials are listed separately.
2Set the distance between them Or press Find a hit to jump straight into the connecting range.
3Read the verdict It says whether the box connects, and by how far it misses if it does not.
